A Typical Class Day

How a day unfolds.

Every class follows a rhythm — from open discussion to live chart reading to your own practice and review.

10:00 AM
Warm-Up Discussion
Students share observations from the previous day's market. Open floor discussion — what did you notice?
10:30 AM
Core Concept Session
Atish Sir explains the day's topic from first principles. Chart structure, flow, or a specific pattern — deep and clear.
11:30 AM
Live Chart Reading
Real charts on the screen. Atish Sir reads them live, pointing to exactly what the concept looks like in the wild.
12:30 PM
Student Practice
Students analyse their own charts. Identify patterns, mark levels, write their own views — then discuss.
01:00 PM
Q&A and Review
Every question answered. Student charts reviewed by Atish Sir. Learning locked in through discussion and feedback.

The Atmosphere

What makes it different.

Concept-Driven Learning

Every class is structured around one core concept. We go deep — not broad. You leave understanding the principle completely, not just memorising a pattern name.

Live Market Focus

All teaching uses live, current charts. No outdated textbook examples. Atish Sir shows you the concept playing out in today's real market — so you can immediately see its relevance.

Small, Personal Batches

Batch sizes are kept intentionally small. Atish Sir knows every student by name, tracks their progress, and gives individual feedback on chart readings during practice sessions.

Beyond the Classroom

The Market Credo WhatsApp group stays active every day. Students share chart observations, spot patterns, ask questions, and receive guidance from Atish Sir and fellow students.
