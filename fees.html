The syllabus

What a batch covers.

The course runs for approximately two months across 52 modules. It begins with market and chart fundamentals, then builds through chart structure and chart flow, the full family of chart patterns (single, dual and triple candlestick formations, plus Head & Shoulders, Double Tops, Triangles, Flags and Wedges), support and resistance, trend analysis, price action, and chart behaviour — finishing with trading psychology, risk management and position sizing. Every concept is taught from the ground up so you can apply it across equity (NSE/BSE), commodity (MCX), forex and crypto markets on your own.

You can read the module-by-module breakdown on the courses page, or see the full overview of the classroom programme on the stock market course in Bhopal page.
