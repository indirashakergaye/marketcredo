Questions?

Curriculum questions.

How many modules are in the technical analysis course?+
The curriculum is 52 modules organised into 8 phases, plus a chart-pattern library of 60+ formations. It runs over approximately 2 months as a classroom batch in Bhopal.
What topics does the curriculum cover?+
It moves from chart structure and chart flow, through chart patterns and chart behaviour, into indicators and confluence, then strategy, risk and trading psychology, before applying the same skill across equity, commodity, forex and crypto markets — finishing with live practice.
Do I need prior experience to follow this syllabus?+
No. Phase 01 starts from the very basics — how a candle is built and how to read a chart — and the syllabus builds step by step to strategy and live practice, so complete beginners and existing traders both follow the same path.
