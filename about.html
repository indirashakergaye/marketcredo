About the Trainer

Two decades of living the markets.

Not just studying charts — trading them, every single day, through every kind of market.

Atish Shakergaye is a SEBI Registered Research Analyst (INH000006086) with over 20 years of hands-on experience in the Indian financial markets. As the founder of Market Credo, Bhopal's price-action focused technical analysis training institute, Atish has spent two decades not just studying the markets — but living them, trading them, and distilling their deepest lessons into a curriculum that transforms how students see price.

With 20 years of real market cycles behind him — bull runs, bear crashes, consolidations, and breakouts — Atish brings something no textbook can replicate: the intuition and pattern recognition of someone who has been reading charts every single day for two decades. He has built this 20-year journey into a structured programme that teaches you the core concepts of technical analysis from absolute first principles.

No signals. Just the skill to see.
